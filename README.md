# Sargis Ninja — Ninja Heroes: The Dark Tower

A browser game made for Sargis (age 7) and his friends. Open `index.html` in any browser, on a computer or a phone turned sideways.

- 1 or 2 players, playing together
- Story chapters, Mortal Kombat–style boss fights, races, and a Safe House where the heroes rest and drink the tea they collected
- 24 heroes, including Sargis and his friend Vahan (Lu Kang), drawn from their own videos
- English (default) and Armenian

A fan-made game for kids. Not an official LEGO, Marvel, DC, Nintendo, Ubisoft or Nickelodeon product.
